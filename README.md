# Mix Theory
**Author:** K3theking
**Organization:** Almes Corp
**Date:** May 10, 2026

---

## What is Mix Theory?

Mix Theory is a meta-concept.

It is a framework for resolving incompatibility between two or more things by changing their fundamental parts until they are both accurate to their original source and compatible with each other.

Mix Theory does not prescribe what to change — that is determined by whoever is applying it to their specific problem. Mix Theory only prescribes how to approach any incompatibility problem.

---

## Conditions

1. There must be two or more incompatible things.
2. The fundamental parts of all things involved must be open to change. No fundamental is exempt.
3. All changes must preserve accuracy to the original source of each thing.
4. The result must achieve compatibility between all things involved.

---

## Properties

- There is no limit on how much can be changed, only that accuracy to the original source is maintained.
- There is no required order. The fundamentals of any of the things involved can be changed first.
- Mixing can be partial or complete, depending on the goals of the person applying the framework.
- The framework applies equally to two things or more. The process does not change with quantity.
- The outcome of a successful mix is determined by whoever is applying Mix Theory. The framework does not dictate outcomes.
- Reversibility falls outside the scope of Mix Theory and is a separate problem for a separate framework.

---

## Relationship to Occam's Razor

Mix Theory is consistent with Occam's Razor. It is the simplest possible meta-framework for resolving incompatibility. It adds no unnecessary complexity — it only requires that fundamentals be changed until compatibility and accuracy are both achieved.

---

## What Mix Theory is NOT

- **Not compromise** — compromise requires each side to lose something. Mix Theory requires each side to remain fully accurate to its original source.
- **Not patching** — it does not make surface-level adjustments while leaving fundamentals intact.
- **Not selection** — Mix Theory does not pick one thing over another. All things involved are treated equally.

---

## Significance

Because Mix Theory is a meta-concept, it applies to any field where incompatibility exists — science, philosophy, mathematics, politics, law, and beyond.

The Unified Field Theory problem in physics is one example of an incompatibility problem that Mix Theory provides a framework for approaching. Einstein and Hawking attempted to solve this problem for decades without a guiding meta-framework. Mix Theory is that framework. The specific solution remains for those working in the field to determine — Mix Theory tells them how to approach it, not what the answer is.
